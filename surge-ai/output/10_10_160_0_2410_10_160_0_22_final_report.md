3. **Deploy NSE scripts** such as `smb-enum-shares`, `snmp-brute`, and `http-enum` to probe for sensitive services that may be obscured by filtering.

5. **Validate findings with a secondary tool** (e.g., Nessus, OpenVAS) to confirm service versions and identify potential vulnerabilities.
